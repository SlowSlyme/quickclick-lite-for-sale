The product

A local social publishing foundation for a buyer who wants control.

QuickClick Lite is a TypeScript application with a React/Vite client and an Express/tRPC backend. It is packaged as a local Windows workflow so a buyer can inspect the product before deciding how to extend, host, or white-label it.

What is demonstrable now.

  • Unified Composer with selected Instagram and LinkedIn previews and per-channel outcomes.
  • Buyer Demo Mode with fictional provider data and explicitly labeled local sandbox results.
  • AI Caption Studio through an available local neural provider, with an honest fallback when it is unavailable.
  • Validated buyer branding, Post History with safe exports, and System Health with sanitized diagnostics.
  • Separate Instagram and LinkedIn Lite provider paths. LinkedIn Lite is limited to the personal-profile path when the buyer has the approved scope.

Recorded validation evidence.

The commercial staging release recorded on July 27, 2026 passed type checking, build, and a 171-test suite. Browser checks covered the Command Center, Social Connect, Multi-Channel, Post History, and System Health routes. The known Vite chunk-size advisory remains documented; it is not presented as resolved.

Buyer responsibilities and limits.

The buyer supplies their own platform apps, permissions, account identifiers, public HTTPS media, secrets, and paid services. The package does not include social accounts, tokens, customer data, hosted operations, a scheduler, TikTok real publishing, advanced analytics, LinkedIn company pages, or a guarantee of future platform API approval.

What happens before transfer.

Before payment, a buyer can review the demo, screenshots, this brief, the validation record, the package-manifest summary, hashes, and commercial scope. The identified source ZIP is delivered after confirmed checkout payment or an agreed escrow process. This page is commercial information, not legal advice.
